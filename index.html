About me

I am a second-year undergraduate student majoring in Artificial Intelligence at Yonsei University, and an undergraduate research intern in Prof. Taery Kim's research group.

My interests lie at the intersection of machine learning and social interaction. I want to develop AI agents that can take part in conversations naturally—not only by generating good responses, but also by recognizing when to engage, when to listen, and how to respond to different people.
